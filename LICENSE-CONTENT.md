## What this covers

Everything we wrote ourselves: the case files and questions (`questions/`), the research essays, overviews and cliff notes, the card data in `site/data/`, the guides and indexes, and our own summaries and notes on top of the texts we hold.

## What this does not cover

A license can only give away what is ours. These are not:

- **Scripture and ancient texts in public-domain translations.** The King James Version, the *Ante-Nicene Fathers* and *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, R. H. Charles, M. R. James, J. M. Harden and the like. Nobody owns these, and you need no permission from anyone to use them.
- **Translations still under copyright, held here for research.** They belong to their translators and publishers and are **not** licensed by us. These include Thomas O. Lambdin's Gospel of Thomas (also quoted in the Q reconstruction), the Nag Hammadi translations by Marvin Meyer and Wesley W. Isenberg, Cyril C. Richardson's Didache, Edmond Bordeaux Szekely's *Essene Gospel of Peace*, and the attributed excerpts from published Dead Sea Scrolls translations. Each file's header names its translator.
- **Podcast transcripts** (`podcast-archive/`). These are auto-generated captions of other people's shows: *The Jesus Way* (Aaron Abke and James Benefico), Dr. James Tabor, and Kameron Waters. The words belong to the speakers. We keep them as research pointers to what was said and when.
- **Quotations inside our writing.** Short passages from books, articles and websites are quoted with citations under fair use and remain their authors'.
