# Audit Worksheet (open draft)

**This is a working draft, open for anyone's input.** It takes each item on `AUDIT-LIST.md` and puts the evidence side by side, so a person can check it in a couple of minutes instead of an hour. Nothing here has been applied to the research files.

**Status:** started 2026-10-20 by Rex's session. A1 is fully worked as the model; A2-A15 are to be filled in (Processing Roadmap, Next Up 17).

## How to add your input

Anyone in the group can weigh in. Pull the repo, find the item, and add a line under **Input**:

```
- **Your name, date:** what you checked, what you think, and any source we should look at.
```

Disagreements are welcome. That's the point of the draft. If you'd rather not edit the file, send your thoughts to Rex or Matt and they'll add them with your name.

When an item has been checked by at least one person, who says which sources they read, it can move to `FIX-LIST.md` and be applied, or be closed as "not a problem."

## What each item shows

1. **Our current wording:** exactly what the repo says now, with file and line.
2. **The held text:** the passage from the source we hold, with its translator named.
3. **The original language,** where it matters (Greek, Hebrew), or the KJV.
4. **Proposed wording:** the replacement, following the repo's citation rules.
5. **What's still open:** anything a person needs to decide or look up.
6. **Input:** comments from the group.

## A1. The Ebionite sacrifice saying: "the wrath of God"

**1. Our current wording** (five places in four files):

| File | Line | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| `christianity/hebrew-gospel-of-matthew.md` | 5 | "…Until you cease from sacrificing, the wrath of God will not cease from you." Credits "Fragments preserved by Epiphanius **and the Clementine writings**" |
| `christianity/hebrew-gospel-of-matthew.md` | 32 | "…And until you cease from sacrificing, the wrath of God will not cease from you." |
| `christianity/blood-atonement-logical-incoherence.md` | 88 | Same wording, "Attributed to Jesus" |
| `christianity/blood-magic-origins-saturn-sacrifice.md` | 149 | Same wording |
| `christianity/prophetic-tradition-against-sacrifice.md` | 88 | Same wording |

**2. The held text:** Epiphanius, *Panarion* 30,16,5a, translated by Craig Wescoe (`christianity/Incoming/epiphanius-panarion-book-1-full-text.md`):

> "I have come to do away with the sacrifices, and unless you cease from sacrificing, the wrath will not cease from you."

Epiphanius follows it by calling these sayings "the crafty devices among them" (30,16,5b). It comes from the gospel the Ebionites used, which he calls "not whole and entirely complete, but falsified and mutilated (and they call it Hebrew)" (30,13,2).

**3. The Greek** (Holl's edition, via First1KGreek): ἡ ὀργή, "the wrath," with no "of God." The verb for "do away with" is καταλῦσαι, the same verb as Matthew 5:17, "I am not come to destroy."

**4. Proposed wording:**

> "I have come to do away with the sacrifices, and unless you cease from sacrificing, the wrath will not cease from you." — the Ebionites' gospel, quoted by Epiphanius, *Panarion* 30,16,5a (trans. Wescoe)

Label the source as the **Gospel of the Ebionites (via Epiphanius)**, not the Hebrew Gospel of Matthew. In the Hebrew Matthew file, drop "and the Clementine writings": the saying isn't in the Clementines. (Recognitions 1.39 has the related idea of baptism replacing sacrifice, which probably explains the mix-up.)

**5. What's still open:**
- Where did "of God" come from? It's probably M. R. James's 1924 translation (*The Apocryphal New Testament*) or Frank Williams's. If we want to keep that wording, we'd need to hold that translation and name it.
- Is it fair to keep calling it the Hebrew Gospel of Matthew anywhere, given Epiphanius says the Ebionites' version is altered? Probably no, but that's a call for the group.
