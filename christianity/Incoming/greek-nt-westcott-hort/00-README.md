# Westcott-Hort Greek New Testament (1881): the 27 books, one verse per line

**What this is.** B. F. Westcott and F. J. A. Hort, *The New Testament in the Original Greek* (Macmillan, 1881), the critical Greek text behind the 1881 Revised Version and the ancestor of today's critical editions. Digital text: Maurice A. Robinson's electronic edition, https://github.com/byztxt/greektext-westcott-hort (commit 91473892d7f36f1227e5c24f8d994d1da40311ad (2017-05-23)), converted from Online Bible ASCII Greek to Greek letters by `scripts/import-westcott-hort.py`. Every verse is on its own line as `**chapter:verse** text`.

**Why it's here.** So that a claim about what the Greek says (Mark 7:19 *katharizōn*, the longer ending of Mark, John 1:18 *monogenēs theos*, the missing Comma Johanneum at 1 John 5:7) can be checked against a public-domain Greek text instead of from memory. The SBL Greek New Testament and NA28 are not public domain.

**Read this before grepping.** The Greek is **unaccented and lower-case** (the digital text carries no accents, breathings or capitals). Search `καθαριζων`, not `καθαρίζων`. Final sigma is `ς`.

**Brackets are Westcott and Hort's, kept as printed.** `[ ]` single brackets: words of doubtful genuineness. `[[ ]]` double brackets: passages they judged not part of the original text but printed anyway, notably Mark 16:9-20, John 7:53-8:11 and Luke 23:34a ("Father, forgive them"). A verse number with no text (Matthew 17:21, 18:11, 23:14, Acts 8:37 and others) is one they leave out; the line says so. Mark 1:45 carries a `| αλλα | αλλ |` spelling note from the digital text. The shorter ending of Mark is on the 16:20 line, after the longer ending, each in its own `[[ ]]`.

**How to cite.** `Mark 7:19 (Westcott-Hort 1881)`. If you add accents in a quotation, say that you did.

**Rights.** Public domain: the 1881 edition is long out of copyright, and the digital text's README says "License? Public Domain. Copy freely."

**Spot-check at import.** Spot-checked 2026-10-26: Matthew 28:19, Mark 1:1 (no 'son of God'), 7:19 (καθαριζων), 16:8-9 (longer ending opens with [[ at 16:9; the shorter ending follows 16:20, also in [[ ]]), John 1:18 (μονογενης θεος), 7:53-8:11 in [[ ]], Luke 23:34a in [[ ]], 1 John 5:7 (no Comma Johanneum) agree with Westcott-Hort's known readings and brackets; not yet compared line by line with a scan of the 1881 printing. Not yet audited verse-by-verse.
