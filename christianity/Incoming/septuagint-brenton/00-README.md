# Brenton's English Septuagint (1844 / 1851), one verse per line

**What this is.** Sir Lancelot C. L. Brenton's English translation of the Greek Old Testament (the Septuagint), first published in English in 1844 and with the Greek in 1851 (Bagster), from eBible.org's public-domain text (the 1870 printing, with eBible's documented corrections). Split into one file per book by `scripts/import-ebible.py`.

**Why it's here.** The New Testament writers mostly quote the Greek Old Testament, not the Hebrew. Hebrews 10:5 ("a body hast thou prepared me") quotes Psalm 39:7 in this text, where the Hebrew (and the KJV and JPS at Psalm 40:6) has "mine ears hast thou opened". Questions like that need the Septuagint on the shelf.

**Numbering is the Septuagint's.** Psalms 10-147 mostly run one lower than the Hebrew/English (LXX 9 = Hebrew 9-10; LXX 113 = 114-115; LXX 114-115 = 116; LXX 146-147 = 147); Jeremiah 25-51 are in the Septuagint order (Brenton's appendix tables the correspondence); a few books (Esther, Daniel, Proverbs 24-31) are arranged differently. Always give the Brenton number, and the English one where it differs.

**Brackets.** `[ ]` marks words Brenton supplied, which the printed edition sets in italics. Brenton's footnotes are not included.

**File order.** The books of the Hebrew canon in English Old Testament order (Greek Esther and Greek Daniel in their usual places), then the other books of Brenton's edition in eBible's order.

**How to cite.** `Hosea 6:6 (Brenton)`. Quote this file's wording.

**Source.** https://ebible.org/Scriptures/details.php?id=eng-Brenton (eBible source files dated 2026-10-07). **Rights:** public domain.

**Spot-check at import.** Spot-checked 2026-10-26: Hosea 6:6, Isaiah 53:12, Psalm 39:7 (= Hebrew/English Psalm 40:6), Daniel 7:13, Jeremiah 38:31 (= English 31:31) read as expected; not yet compared with a scan of the printed edition. Not yet audited verse-by-verse.
