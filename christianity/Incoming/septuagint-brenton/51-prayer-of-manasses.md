# Prayer of Manasses (Brenton's English Septuagint)

**Attribution:** Prayer of Manasses. Traditional attribution; see mainstream scholarship for authorship and dating of the book itself
**Translation:** Sir Lancelot Charles Lee Brenton, *The Septuagint Version of the Old Testament* (English, London: Samuel Bagster and Sons, 1844; with the Greek, 1851), "according to the Vatican text". eBible.org's text is the 1870 Bagster printing with corrections it documents in its errata
**Original Language:** Greek (the Septuagint), translated from Hebrew (and Aramaic) 3rd-1st century BCE; some of the additional books were composed in Greek
**Estimated Date:** Translation 1844 / 1851; the underlying book is dated by scholarship, not by this file
**Source:** https://ebible.org/Scriptures/details.php?id=eng-Brenton (VPL download https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-Brenton_vpl.zip, eBible source files dated 2026-10-07), imported by `scripts/import-ebible.py` on 2026-10-26; one verse per line so that exact phrases grep on a single line

**Note:** Not in the Hebrew canon; part of the Septuagint and of the Catholic and Orthodox Old Testaments. Translated from the Greek Septuagint, not the Hebrew, so it often reads differently from the KJV and JPS; that difference is the point of holding it (the New Testament usually quotes the Septuagint). **Numbering is the Septuagint's as Brenton prints it:** Psalms 10-147 are mostly one lower than the Hebrew/English (Psalm 39:7 here is Psalm 40:6 in English, and Psalm 39 has the superscription as verse 1), and Jeremiah 25-51 follow the Septuagint order (Jeremiah 38:31 here is the new-covenant passage, English 31:31). `[ ]` marks words Brenton supplied (printed in italics); his footnotes are not included. ## Prayer of Manasses 1

**1:1** O Lord Almighty, the God of our fathers Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and of their righteous seed;
**1:2** that hast made the heaven and the earth with all their adornment;
**1:3** that hast bound the sea with the word of thy commandment; that hast closed the abyss and sealed it with thy fearful and glorious name;
**1:4** whom all things revere and tremble before the face of thy power,
**1:5** because the magnificence of thy glory is unendurable and irresistible the wrath of thy threatening against sinners:
**1:6** the mercy of thy promise is both immeasurable and inscrutable;
**1:7** for thou art the Lord most high, compassionate, longsuffering, and most merciful, repenting of the evils of men. Thou, Lord, according to the abundance of thy goodness, hast proclaimed repentance and forgiveness to those that have sinned against thee, and in the multitude of thy kindnesses thou hast decreed for sinners repentance unto salvation.
**1:8** Surely thou, O Lord, the God of the just, hast not appointed repentance for the just, for Abraham and Isaac and Jacob who have not sinned against thee; but thou hast appointed repentance for me a sinner:
**1:9** for I have sinned above the number of the sand of the sea. My transgressions are multiplied, O Lord, they are multiplied, and I am not worthy to look at or see the height of heaven, for the multitude of my iniquities,
**1:10** being bowed down by many iron bonds, so that I cannot uplift my head, and there is no release for me, because I have provoked thy anger, and have done evil before thee, not doing thy will, nor keeping thy commandments, but setting up abominations and multiplying offences.
**1:11** And now I bend the knee of my heart, beseeching thy goodness:
**1:12** I have sinned, Lord, I have sinned, and I acknowledge my transgressions:
**1:13** but I pray and beseech thee, release me, Lord, release me, and destroy me not with my transgressions; keep not evils for me in anger for ever, nor condemn me to the lowest parts of the earth: because thou art God, the God of the repenting;
**1:14** and in me thou wilt shew all thy benevolence, for that me unworthy thou wilt save, according to thy great mercy:
**1:15** and I will praise thee continually all the days of my life: for all the host of the heavens sings to thee, and thine is the glory for ever and ever. Amen.
