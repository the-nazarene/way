### C77 · Acts 2:38, 8:16, 10:48, 19:5 (baptism formulas)
**Claim:** "Every baptism recorded in Acts is in the name of Yeshua."
**Result:** Reads differently
**Held text:** `christianity/Incoming/kjv/44-acts.md` (KJV)
**Text says:**
> be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins
> only they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.
> And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of the Lord.
> When they heard this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.
**Note:** Four baptism scenes in Acts state a formula, and all four name Jesus or "the Lord"; none uses the triadic formula. About five more baptisms state no formula at all. So "every baptism that states a formula" holds, and "every baptism" is broader than the text. In the KJV, 10:48 reads "in the name of the Lord", not "Jesus".

### C177 · Luke 22:15-16
**Claim:** Even Luke's Yeshua says he will not eat the Passover; "the two versions agree that Yeshua did not eat the Passover lamb. They differ on whether he wanted to."
**Result:** Reads differently
**Held text:** `christianity/Incoming/kjv/42-luke.md` (KJV)
**Text says:**
> And he said unto them, With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer:
> For I say unto you, I will not any more eat thereof, until it be fulfilled in the kingdom of God.
**Note:** The KJV's "not any more" (the Textus Receptus adds *ouketi*) reads as if he is eating it now. Westcott-Hort has only ου μη φαγω αυτο, "I will not eat it" (C185), which is the text the sheet's argument needs. Even there, 22:15 says he desired to eat "this passover", and Epiphanius, the only witness to the Ebionite form, says "he both kept the Passover and ate meat" (Panarion 30.22.5c; see C49). "Did not eat the lamb" is a reading of Luke, not something either text says.

**Checked:** 2026-10-28, Claude (`audits/2026-10-28-florez-hebrew-gospel-citation-check.md`); person check: not yet
