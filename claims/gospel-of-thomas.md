# Claims: the Gospel of Thomas

Held text: `christianity/Incoming/gospel-of-thomas-full-text.md` (Coptic, Thomas O. Lambdin's translation). We do not hold the Greek fragments (P.Oxy 1, 654, 655) or the Patterson-Meyer Scholars Version; claims that depend on them say so.

### C57 · Gospel of Thomas 2
**Claim:** Thomas 2 has the full chain, "the same chain, seek, find, marvel, reign, rest", as Clement's quotation from the Gospel according to the Hebrews.
**Result:** Reads differently
**Held text:** `christianity/Incoming/gospel-of-thomas-full-text.md` (Lambdin)
**Text says:**
> Let him who seeks continue seeking until he finds. When he finds, he will become troubled. When he becomes troubled, he will be astonished, and he will rule over the All.
**Note:** The Coptic in Lambdin runs seek, find, troubled, astonished, rule, with no "rest". "Rest" is in the Greek form (P.Oxy 654), which we don't hold. "Thomas 2 in its Greek form (P.Oxy 654)" would be the accurate way to cite the five-step chain.

### C75 · Gospel of Thomas (as a whole)
**Claim:** Thomas is "a list of sayings, each opening 'Jesus said'".
**Result:** Reads differently
**Held text:** `christianity/Incoming/gospel-of-thomas-full-text.md` (Lambdin)
**Text says:**
> (27) "If you do not fast as regards the world
> (104) They said to Jesus, "Come, let us pray today and let us fast."
**Note:** Most sayings open "Jesus said", but not all. Saying 1 opens "And he said", 27 and 101 open straight with the saying, and several open with a question from the disciples or others (12, 99, 104). A general description rather than a quotation, so this is a small point.
