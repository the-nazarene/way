# Claims about works we don't hold yet

These claims stay at reference level (cited, not quoted) until the work is added and registered in `VERIFIED-SOURCES.md`. When a work is added, move its entries to that work's file in `claims/`, check them, and change the result.

Most of these are not in the public-domain ANF/NPNF volumes, so they will need another edition. Jerome's commentaries, for example, were never translated in NPNF. `PROCESSING-ROADMAP.md` Next Up 20 lists the sources we plan to add.

### C158 · Tertullian, "there was a time when the Son was not"
**Claim:** "Tertullian still says in *Against Praxeas* that 'there was a time when the Son was not' in the sense that the Word was not yet begotten as Son."
**Result:** Not held
**Note:** The sentence usually quoted ("there was a time when... the Son was not", *fuit autem tempus cum... filius non fuit*) is from *Against Hermogenes* 3, not *Against Praxeas*. *Against Praxeas* 5-7 has the related idea that the Word went forth as Son before creation, but not that sentence. Check the reference in an edition before quoting it under *Praxeas*.

### C163 · Hosius of Cordoba and homoousios at Nicaea
**Claim:** The council adopted *homoousios* "proposed by the emperor's adviser Hosius of Cordoba".
**Result:** Not held
**Note:** No source we hold says this. The ancient evidence is indirect: Philostorgius (*Church History* 1.7, an Arian historian, preserved in excerpts) says Alexander and Hosius agreed on the word before the council; Eusebius's letter (C157) says Constantine himself urged it; Athanasius says Hosius "put forth" the faith at Nicaea. "Proposed by Hosius" is a common scholarly inference, so say "probably at the urging of Hosius" or "urged by Constantine".
**Raised by:** `extended-library/polemic-answers-was-yeshua-god-florez.md:951`
**Checked:** 2026-10-28, Claude (`audits/2026-10-28-florez-was-yeshua-god-citation-check.md`); person check: not yet
