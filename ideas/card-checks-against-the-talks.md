# Checking the cards against the talks (plan, not started)

2026-10-21 · Matt, for Rex's OK.

**Why:** all ~1,000 talk files are now searchable (search.nazareneway.com), but only 4 of the 14 cards cite anything from them. Ten scholars' worth of talks could strengthen or challenge every card, and nobody has looked yet.

**How:** go claim by claim, through the audit list Rex already uses (the same method as the Panarion audit). One card at a time, **Paul first**: it's the most contested card and has the most talk coverage.

1. **Split the card into its claims.** A card has roughly 10 to 20.
2. **Search all the talks for each claim**, using the semantic index. Tagged or not, every file is covered. (Only 411 of the 1,003 talk files have topic tags, so the tags alone would miss most of them.)
3. **The model filters:**
   - It sets aside everything that agrees with the card.
   - It merges repeats: Tabor making the same point in six talks counts as one item.
   - It keeps only the **5 to 10 strongest** challenges or pieces of evidence the card is missing.

   Each item comes with the scholar's timestamp, the card's current wording, and the primary passage we hold.
4. **The items go onto `AUDIT-LIST.md`** as Needs audit or Evidence in hand, and new evidence goes into a short proposals file. A person checks each one, about 5 to 10 minutes per item, so roughly 1 to 2 hours per card. Nothing on a card changes until a person signs off.

**Rules kept:** transcripts are pointers, not proof, so every item rests on the primary text, never on the talk. The site's promise, "every card was written and checked by a person," still holds.

**If the checking turns out tedious, or the items turn out weak,** we stop or find another way. Search alone still delivers most of the value.

**Later and separate:** use the topic tags to scout new cards the talks cover heavily: Mary, Jesus's family, archaeology, and the last week.
