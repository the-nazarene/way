## Key Teachings

### 1. Clothing, fruit and the false prophet
The host builds the Colossians 3:12 image of "clothing" from Isaiah 61:10 ("garments of salvation," "robe of righteousness"), 1 Peter 3:3-4 (the "gentle and quiet spirit"), and Matthew 7:15-20 (the wolf in sheep's clothing, known "by their fruits"). The argument is that Christians should "clothe" themselves with Christ's character, not with its opposite. Using Romans 13:14 ("put on... the Lord Jesus Christ and make no provision for the flesh"), the host says that resisting appetite for animal products is part of putting on Christ.

### 2. Holiness and the Spirit as God's temple
Holiness is grounded in 1 Peter 1:15-16, which the host says quotes Leviticus 11 ("be holy for I am Holy"). He reads 1 Corinthians 3:16-17 as making believers the temple where God's Spirit dwells, in contrast to the stone temples. Acts 1:8 is read as a geographic progression (Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, the ends of the earth) that gives a model for widening outreach. The host adds an aside, linking the "pearl of great price" to Simon the Sorcerer, that the gift must be sought with total commitment.

### 3. Love as the first fruit, extended to animals
Galatians 5:22-23 is read as nine fruits, with love first. The host links this to John 3:16, 1 John 4:19 ("God first loved us") and John 13:34-35 ("by this all will know that you are my disciples"). His argument is that if God's love extends to animals, the disciple's love should too. He frames animal-related questions as tests of whether one bears the Spirit's fruit, and he previews a live talk on Galatians 5:22-23 applied to animals.

### 4. The five virtues applied to animals
Each virtue in Colossians 3:12 gets a text and an application:
- **Compassion**: Mark 6:34 (Jesus "moved with compassion" for sheep without a shepherd).
- **Kindness**: Proverbs 12:10 ("a righteous man regards the life of his animal"). The host lists dairy, flesh, fur and leather as "cruelty" and asks listeners to refuse them.
- **Humility**: Micah 6:8 ("walk humbly with your God") and Hosea 6:6 ("I desire mercy not sacrifice").
- **Gentleness**: 2 Timothy 2:24-26, read as freeing people "snared" by the devil, with the reminder that a servant must be "gentle to all."
- **Patience**: Galatians 6:9 ("let us not grow weary while doing good"). The host notes the cost of rejection and urges persistence.

### 5. Lent, diet and the peaceable kingdom
The host recommends that those observing Lent give up animal products for 40 days, referencing the "Daniel Fast" (Daniel 1) and Genesis 1:29 as a diet of plants and fruit. He presents this as a preparation for Easter and as a model that may continue afterward. Isaiah 11:6-9 is cited for a future peaceable kingdom where animals and humans are not hurting or destroying. He says that "we look forward to that day."

### 6. Answers to objections (Q&A)
- **Ten Commandments**: The host says the Sabbath commandment (Exodus 20) explicitly includes animals ("even the animals shall rest"), so the claim that the commandments apply only to humans is "not true."
- **Soul**: He says Genesis 1:30 uses *nephesh chayah* for animals as well as humans, so animals have a "living soul." He suggests checking a lexicon.
- **Jesus ate fish and lamb**: He defers this to a future talk on literary redaction, without resolving it here.
- **Weaker brother**: For friends who eat meat in front of him, he cites Romans 14:15, 14:21 and 1 Corinthians 8 (with 1 Corinthians 16:14, "do everything in love"), and says a brother who does not respect a fellow believer's concern "is no longer walking in love."
