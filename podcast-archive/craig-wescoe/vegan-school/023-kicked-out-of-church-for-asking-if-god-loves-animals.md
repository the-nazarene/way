## Summary

The talk asks whether a Christian's love for God and neighbor should extend to animals, and whether that question belongs in a church conversation about following Jesus. Wescoe recounts a church interview that he says began well, with the pastor affirming the greatest commandment and agreeing that Christians should be good stewards of God's animals. The interview turned when Wescoe asked whether Christians should also extend compassion to pigs, cows and chickens, which he says the pastor did not want to discuss. The pastor then ended the interview and asked him to leave. Wescoe publishes only the audio, without naming the pastor or the church, because he says the issue is widespread and he does not want to single anyone out. He then responds to the pastor's objections one at a time.

His main evidence is textual. He argues that Genesis 1:29 shows God giving humans a plant-based diet, so animals "were not intended to be our food." He also cites the Noah covenant as including "all the animals that are on the ark," which he reads as God's covenant extending to all creation. Against the pastor's suggestion that the gospel concerns only people, he argues that the gospel is "good news for all creation because the kingdom is a place of joy and peace and love for everyone not just people." The talk ends with Wescoe restating that position and pointing viewers to an earlier video on whether animals will be in heaven, which the talk does not itself develop.
