Craig's main point is that God gave humans and animals a plant diet from the beginning. He cites Genesis 1:29 ("your food shall be the fruit of the trees and the green plants"). He says that God "created us all to be herbivores so that we're all at peace with each other," and that violence entered later, changing the ecosystem. The captions blur the exact wording at this point, so the phrasing should be verified against the video.

### Animal sacrifice was not God's plan

He says the first mention of altars and sacrifice comes when Noah leaves the ark, and that God's response was to note the human "evil inclination of your heart." From this he concludes that "even animal sacrifices were not [God's] plan." The speaker gives no verse here; the episode is found in Genesis 8, which is the basis for this table's reference.

Craig uses the passage to argue that the faithful should not treat meat as permitted by default, and that the blessing does not make every food acceptable.
