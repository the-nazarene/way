## Key Teachings

### Humanity was created in God's image, and that image is love
The speaker cites Genesis 1:26 for the creation of humans "in his image and likeness" and 1 John 4:16 to say God's character is love. He concludes that "at our very essence we are created in that image of god in the image of love." He reads the command to have "dominion over the whole earth and over all the animals" as an expression of that image of love.

### The serpent's deception was the claim that humans lacked God's likeness
In his reading of Genesis 2:17 and 3:5, the serpent told the couple that God was withholding something, and that eating the fruit would make them like God. The speaker argues that this was false because they "were already like god." He calls this the "original deception."

### Death entered the world through this deception
The speaker says the forbidden tree carried a warning that "in the day that you do you will bring death into the world," and that "there's no death before that." He presents the disobedience prompted by the lie as the origin of the problems "we see in the world today."

### Deception continues to rule the world
The speaker extends the Eden claim to the present, saying "it's deception that rules the world" and that it appears "in politics," "in government," "in business," and "even in the church." The remedy he offers is to "go away from deception and again live by faith in that image of love."
