## Key Teachings

### Blasphemy against the Spirit as the one unforgivable sin
The host opens with Matthew 12:31, noting that Jesus says every sin is forgivable except blaspheming the Holy Spirit, and that even speaking against the Son of Man will be forgiven. He says many people ask what this blasphemy is, and he proposes his answer in the following points rather than by analysing the wording of Matthew 12 itself.

### Forgiveness of others as a condition of God's forgiveness
Matthew 6:14-15 is presented as "the key." The host reads it as making God's forgiveness depend on whether a person forgives others. From this he concludes that "there's only one thing that can't be forgiven and that is refusing to forgive others," because refusal rejects the Spirit of forgiveness and so blocks the person from receiving it from God.

### Mercy measured back (James 2:13)
The host cites James 2:13 as "a very similar idea." He paraphrases it as saying mercy is shown only to those who show mercy, so that a person who holds grudges and withholds mercy "is going to be measured back to you," in what he attributes to Jesus.

### The Unforgiving Servant parable (Matthew 18:23-35)
The host summarises the parable: a man forgiven a large debt refuses to forgive small debts owed to him. He reads the king's forgiveness as a picture of God's forgiveness of us, and our forgiveness of others as the expected response. He describes holding on to a grudge as keeping someone "as our debtor as our prisoner," which he says freezes the one who holds it.
