## Key Teachings

### Water as evidence of where people lived
Tabor's argument is that lead-isotope ratios in plaster from a water system act as a signature of the water that people drank. If the bones match the Sepphoris/Tzippori signature rather than Jerusalem's, he concludes that the people lived around Nazareth and drank from its water supply. This is an inference from a laboratory result the clip does not show.
