# Dr. James Tabor — Paul Playlist Research Archive

This folder contains synthesized research notes for **Dr. James Tabor's "Paul" YouTube playlist** ([PLIRNAKj50kutJrPDYUnC_CJhGPvcEz8rv](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLIRNAKj50kutJrPDYUnC_CJhGPvcEz8rv)) — 77 videos in which Tabor lays out his historical-critical reconstruction of the Apostle Paul. Each file contains a full summary, key teachings, scripture citations, scholars cited, quotable moments, misconceptions addressed, connections to other research, and the full raw transcript at the bottom.

## Who Dr. Tabor Is

**Dr. James D. Tabor** — PhD University of Chicago (under Jonathan Z. Smith and Morton Smith); Professor Emeritus, UNC Charlotte. 45+ years teaching Christian Origins and ancient Judaism. Excavated at Qumran. Author of *Paul and Jesus*, *Paul's Ascent to Paradise*, *The Jesus Dynasty*, *The Jesus Discovery*, and a forthcoming *Lost Mary*. Operates jamestabor.com and the James Tabor YouTube channel. Not a confessing Christian but historically and personally engaged with the Jesus tradition.

## The Core Thesis (gravitational center of the playlist)

1. **Paul never met Jesus** — physically. His framework is built on visionary/mystical experience plus an idiosyncratic reading of Hebrew scripture.
2. **Paul created Christianity** in a meaningful sense — the substitutionary-atonement framework, the Adam-Christology, the cosmic-transformation eschatology, the "13th apostle" claim, and the Mark-as-Pauline-gospel pattern all originate or are crystallized with him.
3. **Paul misappropriated** specific Hebrew Bible texts (Habakkuk 2:4, Genesis 15:6, Genesis 12 "seed" singular/plural, Deuteronomy 25:4 on oxen) to ground a theology those texts cannot support in context.
4. **Paul's gospel is cosmic transformation, not personal forgiveness** — humans being metamorphosed from corruptible flesh to "life-giving spirit" bodies. The Augustinian/Lutheran "save my soul" reading is later overlay.
5. **The Jerusalem community under James** (Jesus's brother) maintained a different gospel — Torah-observant, anti-sacrifice, no body-and-blood Eucharist, no Trinitarian christology — preserved in the Letter of James, the Didache, and the Ebionite/Nazarene tradition.
6. **The Apostles' Creed has a blank space** where Jesus's actual life and teaching should be. Christianity inherited the *that* of Paul (born, died, raised) and lost the *what* of Jesus (Sermon on the Mount, Kingdom ethics, Mary-and-Martha).

Each of the 77 videos circles back to some piece of this framework.

## Notes & Maintenance

- **File naming:** `NN-slugified-title.md` matching the playlist position (#1-77). Special case: `28-...-v2.md` is a stub for the playlist-duplicate of #19.
- **File structure:** Header block → `---` → Summary → Key Teachings → Scripture Citations → Historical Sources & Scholars Cited → Quotable Moments → Misconceptions Addressed (where applicable) → Connection to Other Research → Books & Resources Mentioned → Full Transcript (raw, at the bottom). The same template used by `podcast-archive/the-jesus-way/` episodes.
- **Cross-link convention:** Within this folder, files reference each other by filename (e.g., `08-...md`). Outside this folder, links use repo-relative paths (e.g., `christianity/blood-atonement-logical-incoherence.md`).
- **Build tooling:** the scrape/synthesize pipeline (Apify + yt-dlp scrapers, transcript-append helper, structural audit, one-time migration script) plus scratch TSVs and a `.raw/` working folder were used to build this archive. They were **removed from the shared repo** as operational clutter — the synthesized `.md` notes are the deliverable. The scripts remain in local/upstream history if a re-scrape is ever needed.
- **Updates:** When new Tabor content is added to the playlist (or new related content emerges), add the file following the same template and update this overview's thematic groupings. The synthesis pattern is documented by the existing #01 and #02 examples.

## Project Provenance

The 77-file synthesis was produced over two sessions (2026-05-20 / 2026-05-21) using:
- **Apify** (`starvibe/youtube-video-transcript`) for raw transcript scraping of #1-45 (~$0.44 of $5/month free tier)
- **yt-dlp** as fallback after Apify rate-limited around video #45 (#46-77)
- **Hand synthesis** for the four 2-hour interviews (#05, #10, #65, #73) using Bash-appended transcripts to avoid Write-size limits
- **Parallel general-purpose subagents** for the remaining 73 videos, in batches of 5
- **Phase 4 quality review** (2026-05-22): automated structural audit + 8 parallel content-review subagents flagging hallucinated quotes, broken cross-links, and tonal drift. 11 issues fixed across 9 files.

Total budget: $0.44 Apify spend + Claude API tokens. Subagent retries occasionally overwrote files post-append; the append tooling (since removed from this repo) was idempotent to handle that.

**The rest of the channel** (321 more talks) is in `../dr-tabor-channel/`; its `00-queue.md` lists every video on the channel and links back here for these 77.
