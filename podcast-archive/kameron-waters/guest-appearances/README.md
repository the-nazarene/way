# guest-appearances

Long-form interviews and debates where **Kam is the guest on someone else's channel**, as opposed to `../` which holds uploads from his own channel.

These matter for a different reason than his own videos do. On his channel he sets the frame; as a guest he gets asked things he didn't choose, by hosts and opponents with their own agenda, and he answers in real time. That makes these the best available record of **how the argument actually goes when it's contested** — which objections keep coming back, which answers hold, and which ones he'd want back.

Requested by Matt (2026-10-16) with a specific purpose: so that when Kam opens this repo, it already knows what he has said. His own prior arguments should come back to him as retrievable material, the same way Aaron's 66 podcast episodes do.

## Caveats

Same as the rest of `podcast-archive/`: **auto-generated YouTube captions.** No speaker labels (`>>` appears only where YouTube marks a change of speaker), so in a two- or three-way conversation you have to infer who is talking from content. Names, Hebrew and Greek transliterations, and technical terms are frequently wrong.

**These are pointers, not proof.** Cite a moment as `kam-guest-01 @ 41:12` and check it against the audio — the YouTube link plus `&t=<seconds>` goes straight there. Nothing in this folder is a source for a claim about what Jesus taught; for that, see the source hierarchy in `CLAUDE.md`.
