### The Dead Sea Scrolls as context, not as proof
Scott describes the Qumran community as a group that "removed themselves from Jerusalem," and says the scrolls are valuable because they show "Jesus's world in Jesus's time," not because they mention Jesus. He notes that some scrolls may date to about a century before Jesus, and that Robert Eisenman has proposed that the community might have been connected to James the Just and his descendants.
