## The Series in Brief

SixSeven is Chris's own channel, and it predates Shabbat Studies by three years. It began in 2020 as short explainers "for the not-nerds" (technocracy, 5G, PCR cycle thresholds) and a run of 2021 monologues on trust during the vaccine rollout. From 2024 it shifted to long livestreams, several recorded while driving: commentary on news events (the 2024 eclipse, the presidential debate, the Butler shooting, Hurricane Helene in the North Carolina mountains (16-18), the Los Angeles fires, the 2025 Iran strikes), conversations with friends and guests, and a numbered series from late 2024 built around the biofield and the "internet of bodies," much of it relaying and explaining material from Sabrina Wallace. In 2025 the channel previews a documentary series that maps eight industries converging on what Chris calls the biodigital convergence, with banking at the hub.

Faith runs through the series rather than being its subject. Chris frames the technological and political material as a spiritual contest ("not against flesh and blood," Ephesians 6:12, comes up in several episodes) and closes most streams with the same counsel: step out of the political theater, pray, tend your family, eat well, get into nature, and practice gratitude. Three episodes carry most of the theology. Episode 04 (Yom Teruah 2020, with the artist Daniel Quiñones) explains how Chris came to keep the Sabbath and the feasts and argues for the 364-day calendar of Enoch and Jubilees. Episode 11 (a January 2024 music livestream) gives its long middle section to the end-times reading Chris develops later: "this generation" as forty years, much of Revelation fulfilled in the Jewish-Roman war, the AD count, the little season, and Jesus's return sayings set against Paul's "caught up," followed by Matthew 19 and *pisteuō eis* as "obey unto." Episode 29 (TankTop Theology with Dino V, spring 2025) covers the end times, the rapture's history, a fulfilled reading of Matthew 24 and most of Revelation, and Paul, and points viewers to Shabbat Studies for the longer case. Shorter religious threads appear elsewhere: the rapture as a reason Christians don't look at what is happening (22, 24), Genesis genealogies and Genesis 12:3 (31), the original plant diet (12), and Constantine, sun imagery and the holidays (04, 15, 29).

## Main Themes

### Technology and the body (02, 13, 19, 20, 22-25, 27, 30-32, 34)

The largest thread. Wireless standards (IEEE 802.15 and 802.15.6 body area networks), the Internet of Bodies (RAND, 2020), molecular communication, synthetic biology and smart dust are presented as the build-out of a system that runs through the human biofield. Sabrina Wallace is the main source Chris relays and explains (22, 23, 24, with references in 13, 16, 20, 21, 25, 34). By 2025 this becomes the eight-industry map, with banking at the hub and the biodigital convergence as the destination (30, 31, 32, 34).

### Politics, banking and jurisdiction (01, 06, 11, 14, 15, 21, 25-27, 30, 31)

Elections and debates as staged events; 1871 and 1933 as the years the United States became a corporation; fringed flags as admiralty jurisdiction; the Federal Reserve and an "Iron Bank" funding both sides of wars; continuity of elites from Babylon to Rome (25, with Daniel 2 in 19). The recurring counsel is to stop watching the theater and turn to what a person controls.

### Health and medicine (01, 02, 05, 07-10, 11, 13, 33)

The 2020-21 episodes on masks, PCR, vaccine trials and reporting systems, and later material on food, fats, soil and self-sufficiency.

### Faith as the frame

- **A spiritual contest.** Ephesians 6:12 and "principalities" language (06, 16, 30, 33); spiritual powers behind banking (30, 31); prayer and gratitude as defense (21, 24).
- **End times.** Darby, Scofield and the rapture as recent and as a cause of passivity (11, 22, 24, 29); Matthew 24 and most of Revelation fulfilled in 66-73, with the present possibly the "little season" of Revelation 20:3 (11, 29); in Jesus's sayings the wicked are taken and the righteous remain, the reverse of Paul's "caught up" (11). Elsewhere, Revelation 12-14 read alongside current events (15, from the conversation), Revelation 13's mark read closely (20), and the eclipse declined as a sign (12).
- **Salvation.** Matthew 19's answer as commandments, *pisteuō eis* as "obey unto," forgiveness before the cross (11, as in Shabbat Studies).
- **Calendar and practice.** The 364-day calendar, the feasts, Sabbath over Christmas and Easter, and pagan origins for the holidays (04, 29); a plant diet as the original diet (12, 33).
- **Scripture and Paul.** Jeremiah 8:8, the Writings as "scripture," Marcion, Nicaea, the virgin birth and Jeconiah, Paul as a Herodian, 2 Peter 3:15-16 (29), all of them shorter versions of the Shabbat Studies case.

## Combined Claims to Check

Duplicates across episodes are merged; the episode numbers follow each claim. Each episode file has the full wording and notes.

### A. Calendar, holidays and practice

1. **Hillel II changed the calendar around Nicaea (320-325) and "added a day"** (04). Tradition dates the fixed calendar to 358/9 CE; it is lunisolar, not 365-day.
2. **"Hillel" as a name for Satan** (04). The Hebrew of Isaiah 14:12 is *helel*, a different word.
3. **Enoch's year begins at the spring equinox** (04). Supported by 1 Enoch 72 and Jubilees 6 (held); see Shabbat claim 159.
4. **Jesus kept the 364-day calendar** (04). A scholarly hypothesis (Jaubert), not a consensus.
5. **Yom Teruah falls on the fourteenth of the seventh month** (04). Leviticus 23:24 gives the first.
6. **Jesus born at Sukkot** (04). A view held in several Sabbath-keeping circles, usually argued from Luke 1's priestly course of Abijah and John 1:14 ("dwelt"); no text gives the date.
7. **Christmas, Easter and Halloween as pagan holidays; Easter as Ishtar's day; Constantine and Sunday; the halo and sun imagery** (04, 15, 29). See Shabbat claims 26 and 167 and `christianity/DRAFT-sabbath-to-sunday.md`; the etymology of Easter is usually traced to Eostre (Bede), not Ishtar.
8. **Saturnalia gingerbread and cannibalism** (04). No source given.
9. **Noah at the flood was 535** (04). Genesis 7:6 says 600.
10. **Images of Jesus modeled on Cesare Borgia** (15). A popular claim; images of a bearded Christ predate Borgia by a millennium.
11. **Yeshua and his apostles taught the original plant diet; the grinding jaw** (12). See `christianity/biblical-case-for-vegetarian-jesus.md`.

### B. Scripture, canon and the early church

12. **Jeremiah 8:8, "lying pen"** (29). KJV: "the pen of the scribes is in vain"; "lying pen" is the ESV and similar.
13. **"Scripture" meant only the Writings** (29). Luke 24:27 and 24:44 are the usual reply; same as the Shabbat Studies 2 Timothy 3:16 claim.
14. **The New Testament books were put out of order on purpose** (29).
15. **No Masoretic copy before about 1016** (29). The Aleppo Codex is c. 930, Leningrad 1008-09.
16. **Strong was a Mason** (29). No source given.
17. **"Church" has nothing to do with ekklesia** (29). The English comes from *kyriakon*; the translation choice is the question.
18. **Nicaea set out a "bi-deity"; the Trinity is Babylonian** (04, 29). Nicaea's creed names the Father and Son and adds only "and in the Holy Spirit"; see Shabbat Studies question 11 and `christianity/trinity-fourth-century-construction.md`.
19. **The virgin birth dates from 310-330** (29). Matthew 1 and Luke 1, Ignatius and Justin are earlier; the Ebionite denial is held (Panarion 30).
20. **The Syriac "Joseph begat Jesus" reading, removed by Rome** (29). The Sinaitic Syriac variant at Matthew 1:16 is real; the removal is his reading.
21. **Jeconiah's curse and Matthew's genealogy** (29). Jeremiah 22:30 against Matthew 1:11-12; a known problem with known answers.
22. **Barnabas wrote Hebrews** (29). Tertullian said so; most scholars leave the author unknown.
23. **Marcion's canon** (29). An edited Luke and ten Pauline letters.
23a. ***Pisteuō eis* means "obey unto"; Matthew 19's answer contains no belief; "go and sin no more" as forgiveness before the cross** (11). Same as Shabbat claim 6 (lexicons give believe or trust; John 3:36 sets *pisteuōn* against *apeithōn*); "follow me" (19:21) is part of the answer; John 8:11 is in the passage absent from the earliest manuscripts (Shabbat claim 160).
23b. **"50,000 denominations"; the Hebrew was burned when Greek took over** (11). The figure traces to the Gordon-Conwell count of about 45,000, which counts each denomination separately in each country; no source is given for the second.
24. **"Rob Peter to pay Paul" records the apostles' rivalry** (29). The idiom is fourteenth-century English with no documented link.
25. **Only Paul says Jesus came for the Gentiles** (29). Matthew 8:11 and 28:19 are the usual reply.
26. **Paul was Herod's nephew; the Damascus voice quotes Dionysus** (29). Romans 16:11 says "kinsman"; "kick against the goads" is in Euripides, *Bacchae* 794-795; see Shabbat claims on Paul's Herodian ancestry.
27. **Jeremiah was the only prophet of his day** (29). Habakkuk, Zephaniah and Uriah (Jeremiah 26:20) were contemporaries.
28. **The Apocrypha dropped in 1826-27** (03). The British and Foreign Bible Society decision of 1826-27 is the usual reference; holds in outline.
29. **Why Enoch was excluded** (04). See `christianity/ethiopian-bible/00-overview.md`.
30. **Nag Hammadi texts are fake and behind the prison-planet story** (34, Chance Garton). See `questions/the-gospel-of-thomas.md` on the Greek fragments.
31. **"All is mind" as an old hermetic principle** (34). The wording is from *The Kybalion* (1908).
32. **Moses written as Musaeus "back to Josephus"** (34). The source is Artapanus, preserved in Eusebius, *Praeparatio Evangelica* 9.27.
