## Summary

A late-night music livestream from January 2024 (he says it is January; the Texas border convoy is a few days old; Vivek Ramaswamy has just left the race), recorded on a Saturday night after a day of Sabbath rest and the night after a first attempt in which the audience could not hear the music. Chris builds a track live around a drum loop he hears as Blink-182's "I Miss You," sings old hymns and patriotic songs, and talks between takes, reading the chat as he goes.

Three talk segments carry the stream. First, the Texas border: a truckers' convoy and Governor Abbott's declaration, which he reads as theater, arguing from more than ten cross-country drives that no border of that length can be sealed and that the managed story serves "the American demolition" and a push to end elections (he cites his earlier series on the vote not mattering since 1871). Second, prompted by a viewer's comment that people are worshiping Trump, the end times: he describes moving, after his wife pointed him to a teacher and they began reading Josephus, toward the view that "this generation shall not pass" meant forty years; that much of Revelation was fulfilled in the Jewish-Roman war; that Tacitus, Josephus and Eusebius record chariots seen in the sky; that the thousand years may explain both the AD calendar and a long period of relative morality; and that we may now be in "Satan's little horn or the little window" (the little season). He says repeatedly that he holds this loosely and does not know. He contrasts Jesus's return sayings (the wicked removed, the righteous remain) with Paul's "caught up." Third, salvation: Matthew 19 (keep the commandments), *pisteuō eis* read as "obey unto," faithfulness as the Nazarene sense of belief, and why his family keeps the Sabbath.

The last hour is music, viewers' home remedies (neodymium magnets on the throat, turmeric and honey, onions on the feet), a short answer on the "pills," a pointer to his wife's channel (Swedish Love) for a viewer starting a Christian journey, the hook "Faith over fear," and a cover of a song by a Swedish artist.

## Claims to Check

1. **"This generation" means forty years, fulfilled in the Jewish-Roman war** (47:26, 1:06:48). The repo's Olivet file makes the same 70 CE case. The war is usually dated 66-73 (or 74, for Masada), not 67-68.
2. **Most of Revelation fulfilled in the first century, up to about chapter 20** (49:55). This needs a pre-70 date; the repo's Revelation file dates the book about 95 CE. Same as episode 29 and Shabbat claim 154.
3. **Tacitus, Josephus and Eusebius record chariots in the sky at Jerusalem's fall** (54:04). Josephus, *War* 6.5.3, and Tacitus, *Histories* 5.13, report armies or chariots seen in the clouds before the war; Eusebius, *Church History* 3.8 (held), quotes Josephus. All three describe a portent before the war, not a return. He calls Eusebius first-century (1:06:48); Eusebius wrote in the early fourth century, Tacitus about 100-110.
4. **We are in the "little season" after the thousand years** (55:36). His hunch, labeled as such; Shabbat claim 155.
5. **The world dates time from a first-century return rather than from the birth** (59:49-1:05:16). The AD count was devised by Dionysius Exiguus in 525 and spread slowly; see Shabbat claim 156.
6. **The Dark Ages may be the thousand years, with the record suppressed** (1:13:51). A viewer's question he finds plausible.
7. **"End of the age" means the end of the age of Judea; Greek uses one word for land and earth** (1:07:44, 1:29:26). Matthew 24:3 has *aiōn* (age), not *kosmos*; *gē* does mean both land and earth, with context deciding. Modern Israel was declared in 1948.
8. **In Jesus's sayings the wicked are taken and the righteous remain; only Paul reverses it** (1:16:13, 1:18:42). Matches `rapture-end-times.md` §1-2 on Matthew 13 and 24; John 14:2-3 is the usual counter-text.
9. **The parable of the talents: the third servant "burned it in the ground"** (1:16:13; the captions may be garbled). KJV Matthew 25:18 and 25:25: he "digged in the earth" and hid it; both other servants doubled their money.
10. **Uncertain who wrote Thessalonians** (1:17:12). 1 Thessalonians is almost universally accepted as Paul's; 2 Thessalonians is the disputed one.
11. **"Some of y'all who are here will see"** (52:24). Matthew 16:28; Mark 9:1; Luke 9:27.
12. **Satan, Leviathan and Azazel bound in the abyss** (58:47). Revelation 20:2-3 binds the dragon, Satan; Azazel's binding is 1 Enoch 10:4-5 (held); Leviathan is not named in Revelation.
13. ***Pisteuō eis* means "obey unto"** (1:34:36). Same as Shabbat claim 6: lexicons give believe or trust. John 3:36 sets *pisteuōn* against *apeithōn* (disobeys), the text usually cited for the link.
14. **Matthew 19's answer contains no belief or faith** (1:33:45). Matches the repo's Two Ways and blood-atonement cards; "follow me" (19:21) is part of the answer.
15. **"Go, and sin no more" as forgiveness before the cross** (1:38:22). John 8:11 is in the passage absent from the earliest manuscripts (Shabbat claim 160); Mark 2:5 and Luke 7:48 are firmer examples.
16. **"50,000 denominations"** (1:36:58). The figure traces to the Gordon-Conwell count of about 45,000, which counts each denomination separately in each country; it is disputed.
17. **The Hebrew was burned when Greek took over** (1:36:58). No source given.
18. **Every president but one related to one English king of the 1100s; votes haven't mattered since 1871** (34:25). See the series overview's combined claims on King John and the 1871 Act.
19. **Abbott's declaration and the convoy** (21:04). Abbott used the Constitution's "invasion" language in a January 24, 2024 statement; the "Take Our Border Back" convoy ran from late January to February 3, 2024. He was unsure of the migrant figure ("10,000" or "10 million").
20. **Military-age men flown in on private and courier planes and given housing and training facilities** (25:14-26:45). Something he was told; no source given.
21. **Ramaswamy ran up his company's stock and cashed out before leaving the race** (1:11:40). No source given; the figure in the captions varies ($67 billion, $67 million).
22. **"New money" globally since 2012; Trump aligned with Eastern finance** (1:20:10). From a friend's account; no source.
23. **Polio and smallpox ended through sanitation, not vaccines** (1:25:14). Smallpox eradication (1980) is credited to vaccination and ring containment; polio epidemics grew in the early twentieth century as sanitation improved, the usual reply to the sanitation account.
24. **Warp Speed as a Star Trek reference** (44:23). Same as episode 22's claim.
25. **The Miami mall "alien"** (44:23). The January 1, 2024 Bayside Marketplace incident; police described a fight among teenagers.
26. **Musk as the S in BRICS** (1:23:32). He was born in South Africa; the reading is his.
27. **Neodymium magnets on the throat; turmeric and honey as the strongest antibiotic** (1:44:53-1:53:53). Viewers' suggestions, which he says he will test.
