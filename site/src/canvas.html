            HOW TO MOVE AROUND THE DESK
            MOVE AROUND
            Pan
            Scroll, or hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle mouse button
            Zoom
            Ctrl + scroll, pinch on a trackpad, or − + below the desk
            See it all
            FIT on the toolbar
            Find
            Search the trays; click a section in ON THIS DESK to go there
            CARDS
            Read
            Click a card
            Move
            Drag it; it snaps to its neighbours (SNAP turns that off)
            Select
            Drag a box on empty desk; Shift-click adds one; drag any of them to move them all
            Nudge
            Arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps); Delete takes the selection off
            SECTIONS AND STRING
            Section
            + FRAME; drag its name bar to move it with everything inside; double-click the name to rename
            Tie
            Drag from a card's pin to another card
            Words
            Double-click a string, or click it and press Aa, to write what it means: answers, quotes, same words
            Untie
            Click the string, then Delete or its ×
            KEYS
            Ctrl Z · Ctrl Y
            Undo · redo
            Ctrl S · Ctrl O
            Save to a file · open a file
            Ctrl A
            Select everything
            P
            Present, section by section (← → to move, Esc to end)
            ?
            This help
            Esc
            Close a reader, let go of a selection
            Stamps are a build result, not an opinion, and they travel with the source onto the desk: HELD means the text is in the repo and the cited phrase was found in it; NOT YET HELD means public domain and on the roadmap.
